Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

DronieX Rent Store ("we", "our", or "us"), located at No. 131, D.J. Wimalasurendra Mawatha, Galwadugoda, Galle 80000, Sri Lanka, values your trust. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our website (https://droniex-rent-store.vercel.app/) or reserving camera, drone, gimbal, and audio equipment at our physical store.

We operate in accordance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 (PDPA) and global privacy best practices.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly necessary to process your equipment reservations and fulfill physical rental agreements:

  • Customer Details: Full Name, Primary Phone Number, WhatsApp Number (for reservation slips & automated handover confirmations), and Email Address.
  • Rental Details: Selected gear items, rental start date, return date, and calculated rental duration.
  • In-Store Physical Verification (Handover Only): National Identity Card (NIC), Sri Lankan Driving License, or Foreign Passport number for the physical equipment handover slip. We do not store digital copies of identity cards on public web servers.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your details are utilized exclusively for legitimate business operational purposes:

  • Logging your reservation into our booking ledger to secure your gear.
  • Sending automated reservation confirmations and booking slips via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Contacting you regarding pickup schedules, return timelines, or technical flight briefing support.

4. No Sale or Third-Party Marketing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to any third parties, advertisers, or programmatic ad networks.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses strictly necessary cookies and local storage tokens to store your theme preference (Light/Dark mode) and keep items in your equipment reservation cart active while browsing. No tracking or marketing cookies are installed.

6. Data Security

Our systems use enterprise-grade Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS), Strict-Transport-Security headers, and role-based access control to safeguard your data.
