Sound & Audio Equipment Rental Terms

Please review our safety, acoustic care, and handover policy for JBL PartyBox & Microphones.

1. Store Verification & Pickup

Pickup and functional testing take place at No. 131, Galwadugoda, Galle. Sri Lankan renters must present their original National Identity Card (NIC) or Driving License. Foreign tourists must present an original Passport copy.

2. Weather & Liquid Protection (IPX4)

The JBL PartyBox 320 is splash-resistant (IPX4), making it safe near pools and light mist. However, it MUST NOT be left in heavy rainfall, submerged in water, or exposed to direct sea surf. Drinks must never be placed on the top mixer knobs.

3. Power & Electrical Safety

Charge the unit only with the provided genuine heavy-duty AC power cord. When running on event generators, ensure voltage is regulated to standard 230V AC. Never use spliced or damaged ungrounded extension cords.

4. Microphone Handling & Acoustic Care

Wireless microphones operate on stable UHF band with 10m+ range. Do not drop microphones on hard surfaces or beach sand. Avoid pointing microphones directly into the speaker tweeters to prevent acoustic feedback howl.

5. Accessories Return Checklist

The renter is responsible for returning all supplied accessories intact: (a) JBL PartyBox 320, (b) AC Power Cable, (c) 2x Wireless Mics, (d) 1x Wireless Receiver Dongle, (e) USB charging cable, (f) Protective bags and covers.

6. On-Time Return & Damage Liability

Rentals operate on a 24-hour cycle. Return gear on or before the agreed return time to Galwadugoda, Galle. Physical cabinet damage, burnt speaker cones, damaged wheels/handle, or missing mics will be billed at official JBL replacement cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting sound gear at DronieX Galle.

How loud is the JBL PartyBox 320? How many people can it cover? ▼

The JBL PartyBox 320 delivers high-power JBL Pro Sound with dual 6.5" woofers and 1" tweeters. It easily covers 50 to 100+ people in outdoor beach villas, gardens, pool areas, or indoor party halls with deep bass you can physically feel.

Can I connect an acoustic or electric guitar to the speaker? ▼

Yes! The JBL PartyBox 320 has a dedicated 6.35mm (1/4") instrument/guitar input with independent gain control. You can plug in an acoustic or electric guitar and play live along with Bluetooth background music.

How do the JBL Wireless Dual Microphones work? ▼

It is 100% plug-and-play. You simply plug the small rechargeable wireless receiver dongle into the PartyBox's mic port and switch on the two microphones. They auto-pair instantly with zero setup required and have over 10 meters of wireless freedom.
