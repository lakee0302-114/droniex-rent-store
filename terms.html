Equipment Rental Agreement

Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Physical Handover & Identification Requirements

All equipment rentals are handed over physically at our Galle store (No. 131, D.J. Wimalasurendra Mawatha, Galwadugoda, Galle). The hirer must present one of the following valid physical original identification documents upon collection:

  • Original National Identity Card (NIC)
  • Sri Lankan Driving License
  • Valid Passport (for international visitors and tourists)

A physical Handover Agreement is signed by both parties after joint physical inspection of camera optics, gimbal motors, battery health, and accessories.

2. Rental Rates, Durations & Payment

Rentals operate on daily (24-hour), weekend (2-day), and weekly (7-day) calendar schedules.

  • Zero Upfront Payment: Online reservations require no upfront credit card charge. Rental fees and refundable deposits are settled in-store at collection via cash, bank transfer, or card.
  • Return Time: Equipment must be returned to the store on or before 8:00 PM on the agreed return date.
  • Late Return Fees: To maintain bookings for subsequent customers, late returns are subject to regular daily rental rates (Rs. 1,800/day for drones, Rs. 1,200/day for mobile gimbals, Rs. 2,000/day for sound equipment).

3. Care of Equipment & Responsibility

The hirer agrees to handle all equipment with reasonable care and operate gear strictly within safe limits:

  • Equipment is delivered fully tested and in 100% working order with factory firmware.
  • The hirer is responsible for accidental water immersion, extreme impact, or loss of accessories during their rental possession.
  • In the event of accidental cosmetic damage or malfunction, notify DronieX immediately on WhatsApp (070 187 5455).

4. Drone Flight Regulations (CAASL & Aviation Rules)

For aerial drone rentals (DJI Mini 4 Pro), hirers must observe standard Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka (CAASL) regulations:

  • Maintain Visual Line of Sight (VLOS) at all times during flight.
  • Do not exceed maximum altitude limits (400 ft / 120m AGL).
  • Do not fly inside designated security no-fly zones (military facilities, airports, prison complexes, or sensitive government properties).
  • Prior flight briefing and emergency return-to-home (RTH) procedures are demonstrated free of charge during in-store handover.

5. Cancellation & Schedule Adjustments

Because we do not charge upfront booking fees, hirers may reschedule or cancel reservations free of charge by notifying our store at least 12 hours in advance via WhatsApp (+94 70 187 5455).
